## Description

The Nursing Queen turns feeding tracking into one tap, not one more chore. Start a nursing session, log a pump, or record a bottle in seconds — right from a simple four-button home screen — so you can put the phone down and get back to your baby.

Everything you log stays organized in a clear history you can edit anytime, and every baby profile lives entirely on your device. There's no account to create and nothing to sync — just you, your baby, and your own log.

When you're ready for more, Queen Unlock adds a gentle advisor that learns your baby's own rhythm — general guidance based on your logs, never medical advice — plus stash tracking that tells you what to use next and flags anything nearing the end of its guidance window.

**Key features**
- One-tap logging for nursing, pumping, and bottles
- Gentle, personalized recommendations based on your baby's own patterns (Queen Unlock)
- Stash tracking with fridge, freezer, and thawed-milk expiry guidance (Queen Unlock)
- Private and local-first — no account, no cloud, no sign-up
- Light, dark, and extra-dim night themes for late-night feeds
- Multiple baby profiles, with weight tracking for each

The Nursing Queen offers general guidance based on your own logs — it is not medical advice, and it's never a substitute for your pediatrician or lactation consultant.

Logging, history, and baby profiles are free forever. Queen Unlock — personalized recommendations and stash forecasting — is a single one-time purchase, yours for life, with no subscription.
